# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Licensed under MIT (was proprietary / `UNLICENSED`). Every package now carries `repository`, `homepage`, `bugs`, `keywords`, and `publishConfig.access: public`; `validate-packages` enforces them. New `.github/workflows/release.yml` publishes all packages to npm with provenance when a `v*` tag is pushed (needs the `NPM_TOKEN` secret).
- Billing and spend tracking for people, teams, departments, projects and organizations. Guide `docs/billing.md`.
  - New `billing` API group (meters: `createMeter`, `updateMeter`, `deleteMeter`, `listMeters`; rate card: `setPrice`, `listPrices`, `quote`; usage: `record`, `recordMany`, `listUsage`; spend: `spend`, `trend`, `mySpend`, `teamSpend`, `departmentSpend`, `accounts`; budgets: `createBudget`, `updateBudget`, `deleteBudget`, `listBudgets`, `check`; credits: `grantCredit`, `revokeCredit`, `listCredits`; profiles: `getProfile`, `setProfile`, `deleteProfile`; statements: `listStatements`, `getStatement`, `previewStatement`, `closePeriod`, `markPaid`, `voidStatement`; actions `iam:billing:read|manage|record`; collections `billingMeters`, `billingPrices`, `billingUsage`, `billingRollups`, `billingBudgets`, `billingBudgetAlerts`, `billingCredits`, `billingProfiles`, `billingStatements`).
  - Platform meters (root) and organization chargeback meters; per-unit, graduated, volume and package prices with free allowances and negotiated prices per organization; `sum` and `unique` (active users) meters; reported-cost meters. Each meter's monthly total is priced once per billing account and shared out by quantity, so person, team, department and project spend add up to the account's charges. Usage is attributed at record time to the identity, their teams and department (agents to their sponsor's), with tags and idempotency keys.
  - Spend budgets per month, quarter or year for a tenant, team, department or person with threshold and forecast alerts (`spend-alert` email, audit `billing:budget-alert`) and optional enforcement (`SPEND_LIMIT_REACHED`, 402). Team maintainers and department heads read their own spend without a permission.
  - Billing profiles (a project becomes its own billing account only from its parent), credits, and monthly statements with lines, credit, a breakdown by project/team/department/person and a content hash (`billing-statement` email, `TemplateLinks.billing`); late usage for an invoiced month fails with `BILLING_PERIOD_CLOSED`. New codes `BILLING_PERIOD_CLOSED`, `METER_ARCHIVED`, `SPEND_LIMIT_REACHED`.
  - Contract terms per billing account (`billing.setTerms`, root-only; `billing.getTerms`; collection `billingTerms`, audit `billing:terms`): a discount off the subtotal, a minimum monthly commitment with a true-up line, and tax; statements carry `discount`, `commitment` and `tax` (in the content hash when present, so earlier statements still verify).
  - Policy context keys `principal.spendExceeded` (an enforced budget covering the principal is spent) and `principal.budgetsExceeded` (names of spent covering budgets), read only when a document names them; reserved as identity attribute names.
  - Insights: spend spikes (`billing.anomalies`; daily job `iam.billing.detectAnomalies` with audit `billing:anomaly` and the `spend-anomaly` email), showback (`spend({ shareUnattributed: true })` spreads unattributed spend over people, teams or departments by their share), and CSV exports (`billing.exportSpend`, `billing.exportStatement`; console downloads).
  - `iam.billing` (`record`, `recordMany`, `check`, `spend`, and the jobs `checkBudgets`, `recordSeats`, `closePeriod`, `detectAnomalies`), option `billing` (currency, time zone, team attribution, payment terms, statement prefix, usage retention), `ctx.billing.recordPriced` (inference calls land on the built-in `inference` meter). CLI `billing-close`, `billing-alerts`, `billing-seats`, `billing-anomalies`, `spend`. Console: organization Billing page (spend by person/team/department/project/meter/day, trend, budgets, statements, meters, profile, chargeback), statement page, and the admin Billing page (accounts, close a month, credit, prices, mark paid/void).
  - Invoicing in the style of Stripe and Orb. Invoice lifecycle `draft` → `finalized` → `paid` / `uncollectible` / `void` (`billing.finalizeInvoice`, `markUncollectible`; `closePeriod({ draft })` and option `billing.autoFinalize`); invoice items (`createInvoiceItem`, `listInvoiceItems`, `deleteInvoiceItem`; negative amounts are credits, a negative balance becomes account credit); payments (`recordPayment`, partial and over-payments; trusted `iam.billing.recordPayment` with an idempotency key for payment-processor webhooks); credit notes (`createCreditNote`, `listCreditNotes`, numbered `{invoice}-CN-01`); payment reminders (`iam.billing.sendPaymentReminders`, option `billing.paymentReminderDays`, `payment-reminder` email, CLI `billing-reminders`); a printable invoice (`renderInvoice`, option `billing.issuer`). Plans with fees, seats and meter prices, billed in advance or arrears (`createPlan`, `updatePlan`, `listPlans`); subscriptions with trials, prorated first invoices, seat changes, plan changes and cancellation (`subscribe`, `updateSubscription`, `changePlan`, `cancelSubscription`, `resumeSubscription`, `listSubscriptions`; self-serve plans for account billing managers); coupons (`createCoupon`, `deactivateCoupon`, `listCoupons`, `redeemCoupon`, `listDiscounts`, `removeDiscount`). Invoice lines show the tiers a quantity used, free units, service periods and prorations; prices take `minimumAmount` / `maximumAmount`. Invoice amounts are whole cents. Summaries gain `amountPaidMicros`, `amountDueMicros`, `billingReason`; accounts `outstandingMicros`; `closePeriod` results `drafted`. Collections `billingInvoiceItems`, `billingCreditNotes`, `billingPlans`, `billingSubscriptions`, `billingCoupons`, `billingDiscounts`. Console: invoice page (tiers, payments, credit notes, print), subscription/plans/promotion-code/next-invoice cards on the organization Billing page, and plans, subscriptions, coupons, invoice items, payments, credit notes and finalize/write-off on the admin page.

Helpers `teamsOf`, `primaryTeamOf`, `teamMaintainers`, `isTeamMaintainer`, `departmentOf`, `departmentPath`, `departmentHeads`. A team membership copied by team sync counts only through a group membership no package created. `departments.suggestBirthright` / `teams.suggestBirthright` (`iam:analysis:read`) propose such packages from the roles and groups most of a unit already holds by hand, each ready for `packages.create` (console: "Suggested birthright access" with a Create package button). Team membership reviews: `teams.startReview` / `decideReview` / `completeReview` / `cancelReview` / `getReview` / `listReviews` (maintainers decide keep or remove, nobody decides on themselves, removals apply on completion with `onUndecided` for the rest), the scheduler job `iam.closeOverdueTeamReviews()`, email `team-review-requested`, audit `team:review:*`, `listMine.reviews`, collections `teamReviews` / `teamReviewItems`, and a Membership review card on the console team page.

- AI agents as accounts, delegation, and MCP authorization. Guide `docs/agents.md`.
  - `Identity.kind` gains `'agent'` with an `Identity.agent` profile (`AgentProfile`: `sponsorId`, `model`, `provider`, `purpose`, `url`, `protocols`, `delegable`, `maxDelegatedSessionSeconds`, `boundary`, `suspended`). New `agents` API group (`create`, `get`, `list`, `listMine`, `update`, `suspend`, `resume`, `delete`, `standing`; actions `iam:agents:create|read|update|delete`; plan limit `TenantLimits.agents`). An agent's keys (`credentials.create` now accepts agents) work only while its sponsor is an active person of the tenant; sponsors can suspend their agents without a permission; offboarding hands agents to the successor (`agentsReassigned`, or `agentsUnsponsored` without one). Audit `agent:create|suspend|resume|sponsor-change`. New code `INVALID_SPONSOR`.
  - Delegation: new `delegations` API group (`grant`, `request`, `approve`, `deny`, `revoke`, `get`, `list`, `listMine`, `assume`; actions `iam:delegations:read|revoke`; collection `delegations`). A person lets an agent act for them within a scope; the agent opens `biam_dlg_…` sessions of the new kind `'delegated'` (`Session.agentId`, `Session.delegationId`) that act as the person, bounded by the delegation scope, the agent's `boundary` and an optional scope-down policy, re-validated on every use. Agents can request consent (`delegation-request` email template, `TemplateLinks.delegation`). Offboarding or deleting a person revokes their delegations. New codes `DELEGATION_EXISTS`, `DELEGATION_PENDING`, `DELEGATION_INACTIVE`, `DELEGATION_NOT_ALLOWED`.
  - Policy context keys `principal.delegated` (always present), `principal.delegationId`, `principal.agentId`, `principal.agentSponsorId`, `principal.agentModel`, `principal.agentProvider`; `principal.kind` is `agent` for an agent's own key. `AuditSessionContext` and `sts.getCallerIdentity` carry `agentId` and `delegationId`. Delegated sessions are temporary (`temporarySessionKinds`), never recent, owner or root, cannot mint session tokens or assertions, and are swept by `sweepExpired`. `credentialTokenKinds` gains `dlg`; update secret-scanning rules to `credentialTokenScanPattern`.
  - Human-in-the-loop confirmations: a delegation's `confirm` action patterns hold those actions back until the person approves each call (`delegations.requestConfirmation` by the delegated session, `decideConfirmation` by the person, `listConfirmations`, `getConfirmation`; collection `delegationConfirmations`; email template `delegation-confirmation`; audit `delegation:confirmation-request|confirm|reject`). An approval opens exactly that action on that resource for `validSeconds`.
  - Accountability: `agents.activity` (everything an agent did, for its sponsor or `iam:agents:read`), `delegations.activity` (everything done under a delegation, for the people involved), `agents.catalog` (the delegable agents people may pick). Access analysis reports `agent-without-sponsor`, `agent-admin`, `unbounded-agent`, `broad-delegation`, and `unused-delegation`. `@better-iam/next` `IamPrincipal` accepts delegated sessions (`session.agentId`, `session.delegationId`) and agent identities. Metrics gain `better_iam_sessions_live{kind="delegated"}`.
  - Console: new "AI" section with Agents (sponsor kill switch, keys, delegation switch, sponsor hand-over), Delegations (approve requests and confirmations, grant with a confirm list, organization-wide list), and Models & budgets pages.
  - New package `@better-iam/mcp` (`better-iam/mcp`): `createMcpGate` puts tool-level authorization in front of any Streamable HTTP MCP server (Better IAM credentials decided by policy, OAuth tokens by scope, RFC 9728 metadata and challenges, refused `tools/call`, filtered `tools/list` in JSON and SSE); `createMcpAuthorizer` for SDK tool handlers. For delegated callers whose delegation holds a tool's action back, the gate files the confirmation request and returns its id in `_meta['better-iam/confirmationId']`. `tenantId` is required: decisions are made in the organization that runs the server, never in the caller's own tenant, and OAuth tokens for another tenant are refused. Bodies are read with a byte limit and forwarded as parsed.
  - Inference model `fallbacks` (up to five): when a provider is unreachable or answers 429/5xx/529, the gateway tries the next fallback the caller may use in the same wire format, metering the failed attempt and naming the serving model in `x-better-iam-model` with `x-better-iam-fallback-from`. Also in the `inferenceModels` config kind.
  - Delegation tokens: `delegations.issueToken` gives an agent acting for a person (delegated session) a short-lived JWT for one service outside Better IAM (`typ` `biam-delegation+jwt`, signed with the `a2a` card keys; `sub` the person, nested RFC 8693 `act` claims for the agent chain, `scope` from the delegation). Audiences must match `AgentProfile.tokenAudiences` of every agent in the chain (new profile field, in `agents.catalog` and the `agents` config kind). Scopes must be allowed outright by every limit on the session, and may not be actions the person confirms call by call or ones a deny among the person's own grants could touch. Audience patterns match by URL structure (`*` only as a leading host label or in the path). Lifetime is 30 s to 1 h (5 min by default); audited as `delegation:token-issue`; issued tokens are recorded (collection `delegationTokens`, swept at expiry) so `live` verification re-checks the chain, the acting key, the audience and the scopes. `@better-iam/a2a` gains `verifyDelegationToken` (offline, trusted issuers, optional replay check; `DelegationTokenError`) and `createDelegationTokenCache`. `iam.a2a.verifyDelegationToken(token, { audience, live })` verifies in the deployment, with an optional live check of the chain (`DELEGATION_TOKEN_INVALID`). Core exports `readDelegationTokenClaims`, `delegationActor`, `delegationTokenType`, `delegationTokenLimits`, `delegationTokenScopePattern`.
  - Provider-run tool governance: `InferenceModel.providerTools` (`allow` by default, `deny`, or `policy`) controls tools the provider runs itself (web search/fetch, code execution/interpreter, file search, image generation, computer use, remote MCP servers). With `policy`, each tool is decided as `inference:use-tool` on the new resource type `model-tool` (`model-tool/web_search`, `model-tool/mcp:{host}`; attributes `kind`, `host`). The gateway reads tools from Anthropic `tools`/`mcp_servers`, OpenAI Responses `tools` and Chat Completions `web_search_options`, and refuses with 403 `TOOL_NOT_ALLOWED` before the provider is called (a Responses `prompt` counts as the tool `prompt`; non-canonical MCP URLs read as `mcp:invalid`; more than 256 tools or 64 MCP servers are refused); `InvocationCheck` reason `TOOL_NOT_ALLOWED` with `tool`. `inference.check` and `iam.inference.authorize` take `tools`. Exports `providerToolsOf`, `inferenceToolAction`, `inferenceToolResourceType`, `ProviderToolsMode`; `providerTools` is in the `inferenceModels` config kind.
  - Inference gateway endpoints: OpenAI Responses (`POST /v1/responses`, `max_output_tokens` capped, usage from `response.completed` when streamed), Embeddings (`POST /v1/embeddings`) and Anthropic token counting (`POST /v1/messages/count_tokens`, checked but not metered). Responses conversations stay with their caller: `previous_response_id` must name a response the same person (through the same agent) created through the gateway, else 404 `RESPONSE_NOT_FOUND`; `conversation`, `background` and references to objects stored at the provider (`item_reference`, `file_id`, Anthropic `file` sources, existing containers) answer 400 `UNSUPPORTED_PARAMETER`; a continued response must be at the provider that answered it. Streams that end without the provider's final counts are metered from estimates (request size, generated characters) rather than as free. New collection `inferenceResponses` (swept with usage records), `GatewayRuntime.ownsResponse`, exported `UsageFormat`.
  - Access analysis reports `open-handoff` (delegations handed on to any agent) and `agent-denials` (agents refused 20+ times in a day).
  - `agents.suspendAll` (organization-wide emergency stop, optionally by sponsor, provider or model; audit `agent:suspend-all`) and `agents.directory` (the current attested A2A cards of the tenant's agents in good standing, by skill or protocol; collection `agentCards`, filled by `signCard`, swept when expired).
  - Configuration as code for AI: new kinds `agents` (by name, sponsor by email, profile), `inferenceModels` (the tenant's own models, provider by name) and `inferenceBudgets` (subject `tenant`, `{ group }`, `{ identity }` or `{ agent }`) in `config.export|plan|apply` (new `server/src/ai-sync.ts`; change kinds `agent`, `inferenceModel`, `inferenceBudget`). Keys, provider keys and delegations stay runtime state.
  - Delegation spending caps: `spend: { period, maxCostUsd?, maxTokens?, maxRequests? }` on `grant`, `request`, `approve` (`null` removes) and `handoff` caps the AI model calls made under a delegation and the hand-offs below it (checked and metered like an inference budget; `BUDGET_EXCEEDED`, audit on `delegation:{id}`); summaries report `spend` with what the current window used. Console Delegations page shows "AI spend: $x of $y today".
  - Hand-offs between agents: a person may let their agent hand parts of a delegation on to other agents (`handoff: { agents?, depth? }` on `grant`, `request`, `approve`; new method `delegations.handoff` for the delegated session; audit `delegation:handoff`). A hand-off is a delegation with `parentId` and `chain` that the receiving agent assumes with its own key. It is bounded by every delegation above it, every agent's ceiling in the chain, and the handing session's scope-down policy, key scopes and key issuer, and it is checked link by link on every use. Revoking a delegation revokes the hand-offs below it (`handoffsRevoked`), the person's `confirm` list travels down, and `delegations.activity` includes the hand-offs' events. A hand-off is bound to the handing agent's API key (ends with it, never outlasts it), the handing agents' budgets count its calls, and an agent's requested `handoff` takes effect only when the person restates it on `approve`. New policy context key `principal.delegationChain`. `inference.record` meters a ticket as the caller the check saw, even after their session ended.
  - Agent-to-agent (A2A): option `a2a` (card-signing keys, `jwksUrl`, `cardLifetimeSeconds`, `issuer`) and `agents.signCard`, which attests an agent's A2A card. IAM confines the card's endpoints to the agent's registered origin, sets `provider.organization` to the tenant, adds the extension `urn:better-iam:a2a:attestation:v1`, and signs the RFC 8785 canonical card as a detached JWS (audit `agent:card-sign`). `iam.a2a.jwks()` and `jwksResponse()` publish the keys. `@better-iam/core` exports `canonicalizeJson` (RFC 8785).
  - New package `@better-iam/a2a` (`better-iam/a2a`): `verifyAgentCard` and `discoverAgent` check that a remote agent is attested by a trusted deployment; `createCardAttestor` keeps an agent's served card signed; `createA2aGate` and `createA2aAuthorizer` authorize A2A JSON-RPC servers in a required `tenantId`. They decide messages by skill or an agent-wide rule, keep tasks and conversations (and `referenceTaskIds`) private to the caller who started them, refuse ambiguous skills and non-POST requests, filter the extended card's skills, and ask a person to confirm when their delegation requires it (`A2A_ACCESS_DENIED` -32050, `A2A_CONFIRMATION_REQUESTED` -32051). `trustedIssuers` binds verification keys to attestation issuers. `withHandoff` / `handoffOf` (`handoffMetadataKey`) carry a delegation hand-off in an A2A message's metadata to the called agent.
  - React: `useDelegations`, `useConfirmations`, `useAgentCatalog` and `useModels` (`@better-iam/react`). The key that opens a delegated session now also caps that session (its scopes or session policy, and its issuer's authority).

- Organization sign-in addresses, custom hostnames, and regions. Guide `docs/hosts-and-regions.md` (site: Deployment → Sign-in addresses and regions).
  - `hosts.patterns` gives every organization with an alias its own address, like an AWS account sign-in URL: `'{tenant}.signin.example.com'`, with the region if you like (`'{tenant}.signin.{region}.example.com'`), and `'{tenant}.localhost:3000'` in development. Requests on an organization's address are pinned to it: public sign-in calls act in it (`tenantId` may be left out), and another organization's `tenantId`, session, API key, or page is refused with `HOST_MISMATCH` (403). Organization origins are trusted like the deployment's own; cookies stay host-only. `nodeHandler` now keeps the request's `Host` when addresses are configured, and `hosts.forwardedHost` reads `X-Forwarded-Host` behind your own proxy.
  - Custom hostnames (`hosts.customHostnames`): new `hostnames` API group (`add`, `list`, `verify`, `setPrimary`, `delete`; actions `iam:hostnames:*`; collections `tenantHostnames`, `hostnameOwners`) verifies `login.acme.com` with a DNS TXT record (through `domains.resolveTxt`) and returns the CNAME to publish (`hosts.cnameTarget`). New codes `HOSTNAME_TAKEN` (409) and `HOSTNAME_NOT_ALLOWED`. Passkey ceremonies on a hostname outside the RP ID answer `FEATURE_DISABLED`.
  - Regions (`regions: { current, regions, locate? }`): `Tenant.region` (inherited from ancestors; organizations under the root default to the creating region), `tenants.create({ region })`, root-only `tenants.setRegion` (audit `tenant:region`). `tenants.lookup`, `domains.discover`, public sign-in calls, and organization addresses answer `WRONG_REGION` (421) with `region` and `location` (the sign-in URL in the home region) everywhere else; `IamClientError` gains `region` and `location`. `regions.locate(alias)` redirects aliases that live in another region's database.
  - `tenants.lookup({ host })`, and `region` / `signInUrl` in `tenants.lookup` and `domains.discover` results. `iam.hosts` (`region`, `resolve`, `signInUrl`, `allowed` for on-demand TLS checks). Every email and SMS message carries `signInUrl`, which `renderDeliveryMessage` passes to link builders; link builders now also receive the tenant ID for sign-in emails whose payload lacks it.
  - The Next.js and Express/Hono/Fastify/SvelteKit integrations keep the visitor's host on in-process calls, so server actions and loaders are pinned too.

- Storage queries run in SQL: `find()` filters become typed JSON conditions evaluated by the database, with pagination in SQL when the whole filter is expressible. Hot lookup fields (`INDEXED_FIELDS`: session token hashes, identity/group/role ids, email, OAuth artifact hashes, delivery queues) are indexed: partial expression indexes on SQLite/libSQL, one `jsonb_path_ops` GIN index on PostgreSQL, and id-ordered tenant and natural-key indexes. A session lookup among 20,000 sessions on SQLite fell from ~26 ms to ~0.25 ms. The SQLite adapter caches prepared statements. Schema steps are recorded by name in `iam_migrations` (new migration `0002_query_indexes`). Results are ordered by id in code-point order on every adapter. Identifiers (collection, id, tenant, natural key) with an unpaired surrogate are refused (`INVALID_RECORD`) instead of silently aliasing the U+FFFD spelling; reads and deletes treat them as absent. Field values still accept any string; the PostgreSQL adapter stores U+0000 and unpaired surrogates in a reversible jsonb-safe encoding (`encodeJsonbDocument` / `decodeJsonbDocument`), and migration `0002` rewrites existing rows that need it. SQLite and libSQL compare numbers by their JSON text, so integers above 2^53 and extreme exponents match exactly. PostgreSQL migrations wait up to ten minutes for another instance's migration. - Storage operations:
  - Store snapshots move a deployment between databases and adapters, for example SQLite to PostgreSQL. CLI `store-export --output FILE` writes a consistent JSON Lines snapshot with a header and a counting trailer. `store-import --input FILE` loads it into an empty database in one transaction and rolls back on a truncated, corrupt, or refused snapshot. `store-copy --target-config FILE` copies directly. Copying a database onto itself fails with `SAME_DATABASE`. Core exports `exportStore`, `importStore`, and `copyStore`.

API keys and other session kinds, invitations, access requests, usage records, and pending deliveries are kept. Migration `0004_expiry_indexes` indexes `expiresAt`, `deliveredAt`, and `failedAt` across each collection; the sweep walks these indexes once per run and steps over records it keeps. Shared Signals deliveries now record `failedAt`, and dispatched audit hooks record `deliveredAt` (`tests/retention.test.ts`). Swept:
    - user and role sessions, trusted devices, and relationship tuples past expiry;

- Condition operators: `StringNotEquals`, `StringEqualsIgnoreCase`, `StringNotEqualsIgnoreCase`, `StringNotLike`, `StringLikeIgnoreCase`, `NumericNotEquals`, `NumericLessThanEquals`, `NumericGreaterThanEquals`, `NotIpAddress`, `ArrayContains`, and `ArrayContainsAll`. Negated operators and `ArrayContainsAll` require every listed value; missing or wrongly typed context never satisfies any operator.
- Principal context: `principal.kind`, `principal.owner`, `principal.rootAdmin`, `principal.sessionKind`, `principal.groups`, and `principal.roles` are available to conditions and variables. `permissions.identityAttributes` declares typed attributes that `identities.update` and `serviceAccounts.update` set and policies read as `principal.{name}`; `Identity.attributes` and `Identity.description` are part of the public identity.
- Plugin contract: plugins may contribute `resourceTypes`, `hooks.beforeOperation`/`afterOperation` (run inside the operation transaction; throwing aborts it), and `resolveContext`; plugin endpoints receive `deliver` to queue email/SMS through the host outbox.
- Policy variables: `${principal.id}` and any other trusted context key can appear in resource patterns (after the type segment) and in `StringEquals`/`StringLike` values. Substituted values match literally, unresolved variables never match, and malformed references are rejected at validation.
- Reverse queries: `iam.listAccessible` / `client.listAccessible` return the registered resources of a managed type that the caller may perform an action on, evaluating grants once per query. `resources.registerMany` registers up to 100 resources atomically with per-item authorization.
- Temporary bindings: `bindings.create` accepts `expiresAt`; expired bindings grant nothing and are hidden from effective views (`bindings.list` has `includeExpired`); `bindings.update` extends, shortens, or clears the expiry. `purgeDeleted` now also removes expired bindings and expires stale access requests, and reports `expiredBindings`/`expiredRequests`.
- Access requests (`accessRequests` API group): members with `iam:access-requests:create` request roles with a justification and optional duration; reviewers with `iam:access-requests:review` approve (creating bindings under their own grant authority, subject to `iam:bindings:create` on each role) or deny; requesters cancel; pending requests expire after `accessRequests.lifetimeMs` (default 7 days).
- Events and webhooks: every audit event is now an event. `iam.events.subscribe(pattern, handler)` and `events.onEvent` receive committed events from the dispatcher (`iam.events.dispatch`, alias of `dispatchAuditHooks`, now returns `{ dispatched }`). The `webhooks` API group subscribes HTTPS endpoints per tenant (root may subscribe a subtree) to event patterns; deliveries are queued in the same transaction as the audit record, signed with a per-subscription secret (`X-Better-IAM-Signature`, verify with `verifyWebhookSignature`), retried with exponential backoff, and listed through `webhooks.listDeliveries`. `events.deliverWebhook` replaces the built-in HTTPS transport.
- Outbox: `dispatchOutbox` returns `{ delivered, failed, abandoned }`, retries with backoff (30 seconds doubling to one hour), abandons messages after `authentication.maxDeliveryAttempts` (default 25) with `failedAt`/`lastError`, and dispatches in creation order.
- Configurable rate limits: `authentication.rateLimits` sets `attempts` (default 10), `sensitiveAttempts` (default 5), `windowMs` (default 15 minutes), and a pluggable `limiter`; `createMemoryRateLimiter()` is provided for single-process deployments and tests.
- Administration fills: `identities.delete` (tombstones the identity, revokes credentials, factors, bindings, memberships, authorities, and links; `identities.list` gains `kind`, `status`, and `includeDeleted` filters), `serviceAccounts.list/get/update/setStatus/delete` with an optional `description`, `credentials.list` (`iam:credentials:read`), `trust.list` (`iam:trust:read`), `root.listAdministrators`, and `audit.list` filters (`actorId`, glob `action`, `resourceId`, `outcome`, `from`, `to`) with newest-first ordering. `Identity.status` gains `deleted`.
- Internal restructure with no public API change: the server is split into focused modules (`options`, `catalog`, `context`, `events`, `decisions`, `principals`, `operations`, `flows`, `lifecycle`, `federation`, `http`, and one file per API group), the authentication service is assembled from feature classes over a shared base with `outbox` and `rate-limit` modules, SCIM separates its filter, discovery documents, provisioning, and handler, the OAuth provider adapter lives in its own module, `tenantTreeActive` in core replaces four private tenant-tree walkers, and the whole repository is formatted with Prettier.
- New packages: `@better-iam/react` (`IamProvider`, `useSession`, `useAuthorize`, `useAccessible`, `Can`, and the framework-agnostic `createSessionStore`) and `@better-iam/next` (`createIamNext` with `getSession`, `requireSession`, `require`, `can`, `handlers`, plus `createIamMiddleware`), exposed as `better-iam/react` and `better-iam/next`.

- `apps/console`: a Next.js administration panel (`/admin`, root administrators with MFA: organizations, root administrators, catalog, audit, deliveries) and multi-tenant cloud console (`/cloud`, alias sign-in, invitations, workspaces as managed resources, members, roles, groups, policies, resource types, resource registry, service accounts and API keys, account settings with sessions/MFA/linked accounts). Built on `iam.api.*` from server components, the typed client through `/api/iam`, and `iam.require` for enforced pages.
- Fixed `SafeIdentity`/`SafeSession` (returned by `getSession`, `listSessions`, `groups.listMembers`) losing their known properties: `Omit` over the record index signature erased them; they are now mapped types.
- Resource catalog: `permissions.resourceTypes` declares application-owned and IAM-managed resource types with actions, typed attributes, and parents; managed resources are registered through the new `resources` API and resolved without an application callback. Tenants in `tenant-defined` mode register their own managed types and `{type}:{verb}` actions through the `resourceTypes` and `actions` APIs. Policies, inline role documents, boundaries, ceilings, and session policies are validated against the catalog (`INVALID_ACTION`, `INVALID_RESOURCE_TYPE`).
- Breaking: tenant-defined actions no longer use the `tenant/{tenantId}/` prefix; `actions.list` returns `{ name, source, resourceType, description }` objects. Added `actions.unregister`.
- Roles: `roles.create`/`update` accept `description`, an inline `document`, or a `permissions` list; added `roles.get`, `roles.listBindings`, `bindings.list`, `bindings.read` action, `identities.listBindings` (effective roles through groups), `identities.listGroups`, `identities.get`, `identities.update`, `groups.get`, `groups.update`, `groups.listMembers`, `policies.get`, `policies.listVersions`, and policy/group descriptions. `policies.update` accepts name/description changes.
- Organizations as accounts: optional globally unique tenant `slug` on `tenants.create`, `bootstrap`, and `tenants.setSlug`; public `tenants.lookup` resolves an active tenant for alias-based sign-in. Member invitations (`identities.invite`, `listInvitations`, `revokeInvitation`, public `acceptInvitation`) onboard people into an existing tenant with roles and groups applied under the inviter's authority. `identities.create` no longer requires a password. `links.list` returns linked accounts for account switchers.
- `iam.authorizeMany` and `client.authorizeMany` evaluate up to 50 checks in one transaction for UI state. Invitation responses no longer include token hashes.
- Application-level integration test (`tests/application.test.ts`) exercising the HTTP handler and typed client end to end, with compile-time inference checks; the example application demonstrates aliases, invitations, permission roles, resource types, and managed workspaces.
- Tenant lifecycle administration: rename and move tenants with hierarchy, depth, cycle, and grant-authority validation; list and revoke owner invitations; delete pending tenants; retention purging through `iam.purgeDeleted` and the CLI `purge` command, with audit records preserved.
- `@better-iam/projects` reference plugin: tenant-scoped project records through `projects:read`/`projects:write` plugin endpoints (create, list, get, update, archive, restore), including purge cleanup of purged tenants' records.
